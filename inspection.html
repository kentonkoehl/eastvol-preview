Termite or flying ant? How to tell

Termites have straight antennae, a thick body with no waist, and four wings the same length. Flying ants have bent antennae, a pinched waist, and front wings longer than the back ones.

Found a pile of wings on a windowsill? Check the termite box, and add a photo if you can.
