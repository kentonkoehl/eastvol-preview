Termite protection

Inspections are free. Termite tubes often hide between the blocks and the floor joists, which is why it takes a trained eye to find them.

If they find termites or damage, they take pictures so you can see it yourself. Nobody tells you that you have termites without showing you the proof.

They treat with Termidor only. If live termites turn up in your home within the first year after treatment, they re-treat free. Keep your renewal and the guarantee stays in effect, and it transfers to the new owner if you sell the house.

Termite or flying ant?

Spring swarmers get mixed up all the time. Three quick tells:

TermiteFlying ant
AntennaeStraightBent, like an elbow
WingsAll four the same lengthFront pair longer
WaistThick, straight bodyPinched, thin waist

Still not sure? Snap a photo and send it with the inspection form.
